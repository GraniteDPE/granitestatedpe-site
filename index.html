About the examiner

I've spent 20 years as a professional helicopter pilot, logging 12,000 flight hours across some of the most demanding work in the industry: power line construction, agricultural application, Forest Service-rated fire and utility work, corporate flying, and Part 135 operations where I served as Chief Pilot and check airman. That range of operational experience is what I bring to the examiner's seat.

I'm a Certificated Flight Instructor - Instrument (CFII) and hold an FAA Designated Pilot Examiner appointment under the Portland, Maine FSDO. I conduct practical tests in Robinson, MD 500, Bell, and Schweizer 300 helicopters, and hold additional authority as a Foreign Pilot Examiner and Military Competency Examiner.

As a Basic Administrative Examiner, I also handle SIC and SOE limitation removals, Remote Pilot Certificate issuance, Night Flight limitation removals, and ATP limitation removals. I'm based in Manchester, New Hampshire, and regularly travel throughout the Northeast, Mid-Atlantic, and Florida, with other areas available on a case-by-case basis.

Aviation has been my passion for my entire career, and it still is. What drives me in the examiner's seat is simple: I want to see safe, competent aviators entering this industry, and I take that responsibility seriously on every checkride I conduct.

I also believe the relationship between examiner and applicant matters. A checkride shouldn't feel adversarial — it's a professional evaluation, and I aim to conduct it fairly, thoroughly, and with respect for the work you've put in to get here. You can count on every ride being conducted strictly to the FAA's Airman Certification Standards, no more and no less. I look forward to working with you.
