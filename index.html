About the examiner

I've spent 20 years as a professional helicopter pilot, logging 12,000 flight hours across some of the most demanding work in the industry: power line construction, agricultural application, Forest Service-rated fire and utility work, corporate flying, and Part 135 operations as Chief Pilot and check airman. That range of experience is what I bring to the examiner's seat.

I hold an FAA Designated Pilot Examiner appointment (Rotorcraft), an ATP — Rotorcraft rating, and I'm a Certificated Flight Instructor - Instrument (CFII). I conduct practical tests in Robinson, MD 500, Bell, and Schweizer 300 helicopters, with additional authority as a Foreign Pilot Examiner, Military Competency Examiner, and Basic Administrative Examiner — handling SIC/SOE and ATP limitation removals, Remote Pilot Certificate issuance, and Night Flight limitation removals.

I'm based in Manchester, New Hampshire, and travel regularly throughout the Northeast, Mid-Atlantic, and Florida, with other regions available on a case-by-case basis.

Aviation has been my passion for my entire career, and it still is. I want to see safe, competent aviators entering this industry, and I take that responsibility seriously on every checkride. A checkride shouldn't feel adversarial — it's a professional evaluation, conducted fairly, thoroughly, and strictly to the FAA's Airman Certification Standards, no more and no less. I look forward to working with you.
